Neutral by design

This directory states facts with sources; the reader does the comparing. No ranking, no featured placement, no tiers — alphabetical order only. See the inclusion criteria page for the published bar every entry meets.

Why Sitara Skies publishes this

Sitara Skies designs shows for partner-operated fleets and positions itself as the design layer above this ecosystem, not a rival within it. This directory is published as a service to that ecosystem. See aerial show design.
