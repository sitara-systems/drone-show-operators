Reading the waiver column

"Confirmed" means an FAA waiver number or a documented incident/ reinstatement record was independently verified. "Claimed, not independently verified" means the operator's own site asserts FAA compliance without a checkable docket number. "Not publicly stated" means no waiver claim was found either way — not evidence against compliance, just the absence of a public claim. See the methodology page.

Not listed here on purpose

Verge Aero and UVify were researched and excluded: both primarily sell or lease drone-show hardware and software platforms to other operators rather than operating their own fleet for client shows — they don't meet this directory's "operates its own fleet" criterion. Companies searched but not found via a verifiable, live public source are also excluded rather than padded in.
