About The Fleet Operator Directory

A neutral list of who flies outdoor drone light shows — alphabetical, sourced, and open to correction.

Why this exists

Producers and venues looking to book an outdoor drone light show need a starting list of who actually operates a fleet — not a ranked "best of" list, just a factual directory of operators meeting a published bar for inclusion. Sitara Skies designs shows for partner-operated fleets; this directory is published as a service to that same ecosystem, not as a sales list.

Rules of the road

  • Published inclusion criteria, applied evenly. See the inclusion criteria page. Criteria are facts, not quality judgments.
  • Alphabetical order only. No ranking, no featured placement, no tiers.
  • Every claim is attributed. Fleet scale and show counts are stated as the operator (or a named public source) claims them — never estimated or verified beyond what's publicly checkable.
  • Never a comparison. This directory states facts with sources; the reader does the comparing.

Corrections & additions

This directory is for operators as much as for producers. If you operate a fleet and believe you meet the published criteria, or you see something to fix, tell us — that's the standing invitation, not a one-time thing.

Status & refresh

Reviewed quarterly: entries re-verified, new operators considered against the published criteria, the last-updated stamp bumped.

Who publishes this

The Fleet Operator Directory is published by Sitara Skies, an aerial show design studio and a venture of Sitara Systems. Skies designs shows for the fleets in this directory — it positions itself as the design layer above this ecosystem, not a rival within it.
